# 100 Nights — Sprite Assets (Updated Level Mapping)

Individual sprite assets cropped from the supplied sprite sheet. PNG files retain their alpha channel.

## Folders

- `city/`: 5 combined city sprites, without wall structures.
- `wall_background/`: 5 horizontal wall-background sprites.
- `tower/`: 5 standalone side-tower sprites. Mirror each sprite for the opposite side.
- `troops/`: 10 individual troop sprites, two separate soldiers per tier (`_a` and `_b`).

## Level mapping

| Tier | Game level |
|---|---|
| 01 | 0–2 |
| 02 | 3–4 |
| 03 | 5–6 |
| 04 | 7–9 |
| 05 | 10 only |

The final sprite tier is exclusive to Level 10. Tier 04 covers Levels 7–9.
